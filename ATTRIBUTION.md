The [licence](./LICENSE) is plain MIT. It asks nothing of you beyond keeping
the copyright notice in the source, and nothing on this page changes that.
Ignore all of it and you are still fully within your rights.

## The ask

If you put this in front of users, please keep a visible credit:

```html
Credit freeze letters by Kevin D. Klagge, Esq.
<a href="https://stepuplaw.com">stepuplaw.com</a>
```

A plain link, one that search engines can follow, is the whole request. The
embeddable widget renders it for you at the bottom of the tool, so if you use
the widget you have already done it. Write your own wording if you prefer.

## Why we are asking

We built this for our own clients. Letting your visitors use it costs us
nothing, and that link is what makes maintaining it worth doing.

It is also how corrections travel. Credit bureaus move post office boxes and
change what they want in the envelope without announcing it, and we find out
because someone arrived from that link and told us. A site running a silent
copy of this from three years ago is sending people's documents to an address
that may not exist any more.

If you would rather not carry a credit, the more useful thing you can do is
load the widget from stepuplaw.com rather than copying the file, so your
visitors get corrections automatically.

## What the credit does not mean

It identifies where the tool came from. It does not mean Kevin D. Klagge, Esq.
endorsed or reviewed your product, it does not make us responsible for what
your visitors mail, and it creates no attorney-client relationship with you or
with anyone who uses it.
