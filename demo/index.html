Protected Consumer Freeze Letter Generator

Fill this in once and get three ready-to-mail letters, addressed correctly for Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion's protected-consumer freeze process.

Nothing you type here is sent anywhere. This runs entirely in your browser; there is no server, no analytics, and nothing is saved. Never type a Social Security number into this or any similar form — the bureaus verify identity from the ID copies you physically enclose, not from anything typed online.
